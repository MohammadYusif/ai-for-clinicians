# Accreditation notes (SCFHS CPD / CME)

Trainer-facing. Not published to the site, and **no page or certificate on the site claims CME hours or
accreditation**: the certificate is a certificate of completion, as in the deck. Add any accreditation
statement only after approval.

## What the brief established

- SCFHS accredits CPD activities (lectures, conferences, synchronous or asynchronous e-learning) through
  its provider platform.
- A separate **"Specialized Professional Program"** track requires a minimum of 14 days and **does not
  carry CME hours at all**. A weekend course needs the **CPD-activity route**, not that one.
- The route has two steps, in order:
  1. **CPD-provider status** first: yours, or a partner entity's.
  2. **Activity-level accreditation** for this specific course.

## Contact time, if it is asked for

Each day is 120 minutes including a 10-minute break, so **instructional time is 110 minutes a day, 330
minutes (5 h 30 min) in total**; 360 minutes (6 h) if breaks are counted. Confirm which SCFHS counts before
stating any hours.

## Questions to settle before applying

- Which CPD provider will the activity sit under, and who is the accountable person?
- Does SCFHS require a pre- and post-assessment, an attendance rule, or an evaluation form?
- Are there requirements on speaker qualifications or conflict-of-interest declarations?
- Does the AI-tool content (named commercial products) need any disclosure?
- Is the certificate's wording constrained once CME hours are awarded?
